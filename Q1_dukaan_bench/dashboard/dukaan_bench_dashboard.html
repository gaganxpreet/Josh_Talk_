Head-to-head — and how often nothing was good enough

For each poster, raters picked the one they would actually use, or “none of these.” Most arena leaderboards force a choice, which manufactures a winner even when every option fails. In a category where failure is the expected outcome, hiding that option would be the single biggest way to produce a misleading leaderboard — so it is shown here at full weight, in neutral ink rather than as a fourth competitor.

Did we measure anything real?

Reliability of each axis (Krippendorff's α, ordinal). An eval that doesn't publish this is asking to be trusted on faith. Axes below 0.667 are reported as indicative only and never headlined.
