Show the scoring guide
TEXT — is the spelling right?
3 — Perfect
Every character correct and readable. A native reader sees nothing wrong.
2 — Minor flaw
One wrong/missing matra or odd spacing. Meaning still instantly clear.
1 — Wrong
Misspelt, dropped/duplicated characters, broken conjuncts — or correct but cut off.
0 — Fake script
Shapes that look like the script but aren't real letters, wrong script, or no text.
Can't read
Use this freely. Guessing is worse than skipping.
INDIA — does it look real?
3 — Authentic
Looks like the actual Indian setting described. Products, clothing, lighting all plausible.
2 — Broadly Indian
Recognisably Indian but details are off, or too tidy/affluent for the business.
1 — Motifs on top
Generic international image with Indian decoration added. "AI's idea of India."
0 — Wrong or stereotyped
Not India at all, or the Rajasthan default — camels/forts/turbans pasted onto a Chennai or Kolkata shop.
POSTER — does it work?
Ignore spelling here — that's the text score.
3 — Professional
Clear hierarchy, good contrast, readable as a phone thumbnail.
2 — Decent
Slightly cramped or cluttered, but usable.
1 — Problems
Elements cut off, low contrast, busy background behind text.
0 — Unusable
Chaotic, or obvious artefacts in a prominent place.

Which one would you actually use?

Imagine this is your shop. Pick the one you'd post — or say none of them are good enough. “None of these” is a real answer, not a cop-out.
